05 FAV

SOMEONE WORTH REMEMBERING

Shree — demi lvto

Some people enter your life and become a memory. Shree became a part of mine.

I don't really know how to explain what she means to me without making it sound smaller than it actually is. There are people you can describe with a few words, but she has always felt like someone who needs an entire story.

She is the most beautiful woman I've ever seen, but somehow that's still not the part that matters most to me. It's everything behind it. The way she can be incredibly mature one moment and completely childish the next. The way she can be strong and emotional, serious and playful, caring and annoying — sometimes all within the same day.

And somehow, I love every version of her.

What stays with me the most is that she stayed. She was the first girl who stayed with me when things weren't always easy, without needing everything to be perfect first. She didn't just appear during the good moments. She remained through the complicated ones too.

Maybe she doesn't realize how much that means to me.

There are certain kinds of presence you don't appreciate because they're loud. You appreciate them because, one day, you realize how rare they were. Her presence became one of those things for me.

She gave me memories I didn't know I would keep this carefully. Conversations I still remember. Little moments that probably meant nothing to anyone else but somehow stayed with me. Even the smallest things about her became familiar enough to feel like they belonged somewhere inside my life.

I don't think she's perfect because she never makes mistakes. I think she's perfect because I never needed her to be someone else.

She's simply Shree.

The girl who can be mature enough to understand things and childish enough to make everything feel lighter. The girl who somehow managed to become important without trying to prove that she was. The girl who stayed when she could have simply walked away.

And if one day I look back at everything I've lived through, I know there will be certain people I remember because they were there.

I'll remember Shree because she mattered.

This little space on my website is just a small way of saying something I probably wouldn't know how to say properly out loud:

I'm grateful that I met you.
I'm grateful that you stayed.
And I'm grateful that, for a part of my life, I got to have you in it.
